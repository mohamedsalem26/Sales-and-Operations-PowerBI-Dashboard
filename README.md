📊 Sales & Supply Chain Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to monitor sales performance, financial statement metrics, inventory levels, purchasing activity, shipping costs, and lead times in one unified view.
---
📌 Project Overview
This project transforms raw operational and sales data into actionable business insights across core commercial and supply chain dimensions:
💰 Financial & Sales Performance (key revenue and financial statement indicators)
📦 Inventory Management (stock availability and inventory health)
🛒 Purchasing Operations (purchase volume and procurement trends)
🚚 Shipping Cost (logistics and freight cost tracking)
⏱️ Lead Time (order-to-delivery and fulfillment cycle duration)
The objective is to help stakeholders track commercial results alongside supply chain efficiency, spot bottlenecks early, and make data-driven decisions.
---
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI Desktop
Power Query (Data Extraction, Cleaning & Transformation)
DAX (Calculated Columns, Measures & KPIs)
Data Modeling (Relationships & Analytical Schema)
Interactive Visuals, Slicers & Filters
---
📈 Dashboard Highlights
Executive & Financial Overview: Tracks core sales and financial statement figures to evaluate overall business performance.
Inventory & Purchasing Insights: Monitors stock status and purchasing patterns to balance supply with demand.
Shipping Cost & Lead Time Analysis: Highlights logistics expenses and delivery lead times to identify cost drivers and operational delays.
Interactive Filtering: Enables dynamic exploration by time period, category, or operational segment using slicers and cross-filtering.
---
💡 Key Business Questions Answered
How are overall sales and financial metrics trending across the selected period?
Which items or categories drive the highest purchasing volume and shipping costs?
Are there inventory risks (low stock or overstock) affecting fulfillment?
How does lead time vary across periods or categories, and where do delays occur?
How do shipping costs correlate with order and purchase activity?
